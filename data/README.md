# 📂 Datasets

All files are shared by the notebooks; each one reads what it needs with `../data/<name>.csv`.

| File | Rows × columns | Used in | What it is |
|---|:-:|---|---|
| [`data-preprocessing.csv`](data-preprocessing.csv) | 10 × 4 | Section 1 | Country, age, salary, purchased: a 10-row toy table |
| [`salary-data.csv`](salary-data.csv) | 30 × 2 | Section 2 | Years of experience vs salary |
| [`50-startups.csv`](50-startups.csv) | 50 × 5 | Section 2 | R&D, administration, marketing spend, state, profit |
| [`position-salaries.csv`](position-salaries.csv) | 10 × 3 | Section 2 | Position level vs salary |
| [`housing.csv`](housing.csv) | 20,640 × 10 | Section 2 | California housing: location, rooms, income, house value |
| [`social-network-ads.csv`](social-network-ads.csv) | 400 × 3 | Section 3 | Age, estimated salary, purchased (0/1) |
| [`mall-customers.csv`](mall-customers.csv) | 200 × 5 | Section 4 | Income and spending score of mall customers |
| [`groceries.csv`](groceries.csv) | 38,765 × 3 | Section 5 | Member, date, item: one row per purchased item |
| [`iris.csv`](iris.csv) | 150 × 6 | not used by a notebook | The classic Iris flowers |
| [`bank.csv`](bank.csv) | 11,162 × 17 | not used by a notebook | Bank customer data |
| [`market-basket-optimisation.csv`](market-basket-optimisation.csv) | 7,500 × 20 | not used by a notebook | Market-basket transactions |

> `housing.csv` was **not** in this repo before: two notebooks (`05-decision-tree-regression`, `06-random-forest-regression`) loaded it, but it only existed in the R version of the course. It was added from [ML-Classics-in-R](https://github.com/evgeniimatveev/ML-Classics-in-R).
