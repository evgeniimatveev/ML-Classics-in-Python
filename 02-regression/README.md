# 📈 Section 2: Regression

Predicting a number, from a straight line to forests of trees.

> 📂 The notebooks read their data from `../data/`: open them from this folder in Jupyter (the default) and the paths resolve.

| # | Notebook | What it covers | Dataset |
|:-:|----------|----------------|---------|
| 1 | [`01-simple-linear-regression.ipynb`](01-simple-linear-regression.ipynb) | Simple linear regression: salary from years of experience | `data/salary-data.csv` |

| 6 | [`06-random-forest-regression.ipynb`](06-random-forest-regression.ipynb) | Random forest vs linear regression vs SVR on California housing: metrics, feature importance, timing | `data/housing.csv` |
